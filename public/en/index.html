FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Every project is assessed before scope, timeline, and investment are defined. Here are answers to the most common questions about how we work.

How much does a website or digital solution cost?+

The cost depends on scope, complexity, integrations, available content, and project goals. That's why we start with a discovery phase to understand your needs, then prepare a clear proposal with defined scope, timeline, and responsibilities.

What digital services does NeuroWebCR offer?+

We offer professional web development, custom digital solutions, process automation, artificial intelligence integration, CRM implementation, technology infrastructure, databases, technical SEO, and digital support for businesses that need to improve their processes, online presence, or commercial capacity.

How long does a project take to complete?+

Implementation time depends on the type of project, level of customization, number of integrations, and the client's availability of information. Before starting, we conduct a requirements assessment to define a realistic schedule and avoid incorrect expectations.

Do you work with clients in Costa Rica and other countries?+

Yes. We serve clients in Costa Rica and international projects. Our team has experience working with local businesses, digital platforms, ecommerce, corporate solutions, and high-demand projects, including experience in the gaming/gambling industry, where performance, security, and availability are critical.

What sets NeuroWebCR apart from a traditional agency?+

NeuroWebCR combines senior experience in frontend, backend, infrastructure, data, and artificial intelligence. We don't just design websites; we analyze processes, identify improvement opportunities, and build technology solutions focused on real business outcomes.

What is an AI agent and how can it benefit my business?+

An artificial intelligence agent can help answer inquiries, qualify prospects, automate tasks, connect internal systems, and improve customer service. Implementation depends on each company's processes, so we first analyze the workflow and then define an appropriate solution.

Can you integrate payment gateways like Stripe, PayPal, or SINPE?+

Yes. We integrate local and international payment gateways: Stripe, PayPal, BAC Credomatic, SINPE Móvil, and others depending on the country and business model. NeuroWebCR handles all technical integration; the client only needs to have an active account with the payment provider.

What is the difference between a chatbot and an AI agent?+

A chatbot follows predefined flows (decision tree). An AI agent reasons about context, accesses business information, and can execute complex actions. For simple support or FAQ, a chatbot is sufficient. For sales, lead qualification, or advanced support, an AI agent delivers better results.

How does a project with NeuroWebCR begin?+

The process starts with a discovery meeting where we understand the client's needs, goals, business context, and potential constraints. We then analyze requirements, define scope, and prepare a formal proposal before any development begins.

Do you offer support after project delivery?+

Yes. We can provide maintenance, technical support, evolutionary improvements, monitoring, updates, infrastructure administration, and ongoing support based on the client's needs and the type of solution implemented.
